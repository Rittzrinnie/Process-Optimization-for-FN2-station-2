? Why the buzz matters: the vibration is felt through touch, faster than reading a screen. With hands and eyes on the car, the operator learns to treat the buzz as “scan done” and stops checking the screen. If a wrong part gives no buzz, the missing feeling is noticed straight away.
07 — IMPACT

Why it matters

QualityWrong-side camera panel fitted, or a part not recorded after a false scan. Caught later or sent to rework.
Cycle timeSwapping parts mid-cycle eats into the station's takt time.
Line flowDelays at Station 2 can push the car past the zone or stop the line.
OperatorRushed fitment increases strain and error rate on both LH and RH sides.
